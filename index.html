02 — The room

A rooftop built
for long dinners.

MISO sits above Usuma Street on the Summerset Continental rooftop — a dark, low-lit room where the sushi counter and the Thai kitchen work side by side.

Start with sashimi and something cold. Move on to tom yum, a bowl of curry or a plate from the charcoal grill. Stay for another round. The menu wanders from Tokyo to Bangkok, with stops in Italy, India and Nigeria, because a good table rarely stays in one place.
